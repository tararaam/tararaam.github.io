Overview

Why do you remember your first day of kindergarten but not what you had for breakfast last week? How does a storm of electrical signals become a thought? This course is an introduction to the science of the brain, from the ions that make a single neuron fire to the circuits that underlie memory, perception, and behavior.

No prior biology required.

Course structure

The course is divided into two parts. The first four weeks cover cellular neuroscience: membrane properties, ion channels, action potentials, and synaptic transmission. The second four weeks zoom out to systems: invertebrate circuits, learning and memory, the vertebrate brain, sensory systems, and higher cortical function.
