Interactive tools

Here you will find simulations with AI-powered tutors designed to help you build intuition for the concepts and mechanisms explored in the course. Each tool asks you to predict, explain, experiment, and reason your way through a biological process, and the AI tutor will give you individualized feedback that helps you fill gaps in your understanding based on your responses.

Resting potential explorer

Explore how ion gradients and selective permeability establish the resting membrane potential — the electrical foundation for all neural signaling.

Step through the five phases of an action potential with an AI tutor that responds to your reasoning. Includes an ion manipulation mode to explore what happens when conditions go wrong.
