Tara Raam, Ph.D.

Course Instructor

Welcome to NEU C61! I'm excited to be teaching this course. I'm a Lecturer and Project Scientist here at UC Berkeley, where I study how the brain coordinates the dynamics of social groups. I use experimental approaches and machine learning to understand how animals sense, respond to, and are shaped by others around them. I also teach multiple courses in the Neuroscience department, including NEU C64: Exploring the Brain, and I'm passionate about developing thoughtful approaches to AI use for teaching and learning science. Outside the lab and classroom, I enjoy hiking, running, finding good coffee, and spending time with my son.
